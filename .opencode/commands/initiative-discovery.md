Use `$sac-nexus-initiative-workflow` and `$sac-nexus-architecture` to turn a
rough product, frontend, IDP, business API, tooling, documentation, or
infrastructure goal into a recommendation, PRD, and execution plan.

## Discovery

1. Inspect the workspace, branch, local `AGENTS.md` files, related initiative
   and durable docs, package scripts, current code, and relevant skills.
2. Inspect supplied designs, screenshots, schemas, issues, URLs, or source
   documents before detailed questions.
3. Ask one focused question at a time and normally ask 10 to 20 questions.
4. Include a recommendation, reason, and concrete options with every question.
5. Cover only relevant topics: outcomes and non-goals, users and success,
   rollout, UX, accessibility, security/privacy/LGPD, tenancy, data/API
   contracts, performance/scalability, observability, testing, documentation,
   and skill needs.
6. Do not implement during discovery unless the user explicitly switches to
   execution.

After the final answer, summarize the initiative, goals, non-goals, decisions,
requirements, acceptance criteria, risks, assumptions, open questions, file
changes, skills, phased plan, verification, documentation impact, branch name,
commit, and PR title in Brazilian Portuguese.

Ask whether to create or update the paired files under
`docs/initiatives/prds` and `docs/initiatives/tasks`. Do not commit, push, open
a PR, or merge without explicit authorization.
